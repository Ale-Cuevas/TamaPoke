# Legend for Sunnyside World (16px sheet: spr_tileset_sunnysideworld_16px.png, 64x64 tiles, no spacing)
# Coordinates are tile column,row in that sheet.
# char = col,row [col,row ...]        single tile; several = random variants (fixed seed)
# char = stamp WxH col,row             multi-tile object, placed by its top-left cell
# char = autotile "Set" over b [fill c,r ...]  ground terrain with automatic edges (set from the .yy), drawn over ground char b
# A trailing "!" marks the tile/stamp as blocking (pets can't walk there).
# A "weights" list after variants biases the random pick.
